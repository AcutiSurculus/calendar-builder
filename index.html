QUICK GUIDE

Build your calendar layout

Choose a month and year

Select a month from the month buttons and enter a year. Click a date on the calendar to toggle its holiday color.

Place and resize

A fresh layout starts centered on the paper. Drag the calendar to move it. Drag a corner to resize proportionally; use a midpoint handle on an edge to resize only that dimension. Hold Shift on a corner for independent width and height, or Alt to resize from the center. Enter exact values in the numeric fields; use Show optional sliders when you prefer sliders. Right-click a measurement to switch its unit. Arrow keys move the focused calendar; hold Shift with an arrow key to move 10 mm.

Center ↔ and Center ↕ center it on the paper. Fit to margins sets its width to the available space.

Set up the paper

Choose paper size and orientation. Right-click any measurement to choose its unit independently; right-click the paper preview to change the paper-dimension display unit. Position X/Y and margin settings control placement and guides. Turn on Snap to center & margins to snap while dragging.

Use a template image

Upload an image to preview it across the full paper behind the calendar. It is included in PNG and ZIP exports. Use Remove template image to clear it.

Customize the calendar

Layout settings control the title, weekday bar, labels, and date alignment. Size & shape settings adjust calendar width, box height, horizontal/vertical gaps, corners, borders, and date size. Weekday font size and bar radius are independent of the month/year title.

Choose a font and colors in Font & colors. Load installed fonts requests permission to list fonts available on your device when the browser supports it.

Undo, redo, and export

Use Ctrl/Cmd + Z to undo and Ctrl/Cmd + Y to redo. Your progress is automatically saved in this browser on this device. Choose the resolution and whether to export the selected month or all 12 months as a ZIP. The title checkbox controls whether month and year appear in the export.

Preview and appearance

Use the zoom menu to change the preview scale. The Light mode/Dark mode button changes the app appearance; it does not change calendar colors. Reset layout restores the calendar settings and holidays to their defaults.
